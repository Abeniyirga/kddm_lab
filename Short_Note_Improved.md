# Short Note — Olist Data Quality and ERD

`customer_id` identifies a customer record in the dataset, while `customer_unique_id` groups records that refer to the same real-world customer; I would use `COUNT(DISTINCT customer_unique_id)` to count customers and `customer_id` to join orders to customer records. The `order_items` table is the junction table that resolves the many-to-many relationship between orders and products, while also recording the seller and item-level details for each order line. The observed data is stricter than the diagram for the customer–order relationship because every order row has a customer in the supplied data, even though a customer may have no orders. The optional order–order_items relationship is justified by the saved notebook results: 775 orders have no item rows, including unavailable, canceled, created, invoiced, and shipped orders.
